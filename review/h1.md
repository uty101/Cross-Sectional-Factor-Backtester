**The rule.** `ratio = cap / EntityPublicFloat`, with `cap` the pipeline's own market cap (`market_caps` output: month-end close x the count the portfolios use, in the price basis) at the last month-end on or before the float date. Outside [0.5, 20] is a flag; a flag is excluded for the 12 months after the float date unless its reason is `float_scale` (below). A missing float, or a float with no cap at that month, is a null ratio and never a flag.

## Not verified

- **That the pipeline's cap is the right side of the ratio in the 49 `float_scale` rows** was checked against the ticker's own float in a neighbouring year, not against the cover text. Six were read in the text (GE, Exelon, Genzyme, Prologis, Chesapeake, Alcoa Inc); the other 43 were not.
- **The float date.** `ddate` is taken as the float date. For Exelon and Chesapeake the XBRL context is the fiscal year end while the text says "as of June 30"; how many of the 11,199 rows are dated that way was not counted. The cap is then six months later than the float, which is inside the band but is why 1,642 ratios sit between 0.5 and 1.
- **Share classes.** A CIK with two tickers in the map (GOOGL/GOOG) gets two rows from one float, each against its own price and the same total count; neither is flagged, and the check does not know a class from a total.
- **`near_split`** means only that a split of that size exists somewhere in the ticker's yfinance split list (Exelon's 2004 2-for-1); it does not say the split is the cause.

## Open

- **Exelon.** Four years of a member excluded because the filer's XBRL float is twice its own cover text. The cover text is in `data/raw/edgar/10k/` already (text.py fetched it); parsing "aggregate market value ... $" from it would give a second float that the XBRL one can be checked against, and would clear this case without touching the band. Not built: the plan says the XBRL float is the reference.
- **Prologis.** A count that lags a merger by two months is a point-in-time fact, not an error, and the 12-month window is longer than the defect. A window that ends at the next filing with a count in band would be tighter; not built.
- **HAR before 2013-12.** The eleven worst months (caps of $0.4-2.3trn, 2013-01 to 2013-11) are outside every float window because the FY2013 float has no price against it. H2 covers them by the symbol's identity (yfinance says HAR is a "YHD" fund, quote type MUTUALFUND, short name "906601").

## Against the plan

- **Source.** The plan puts the float in `tag_map.toml`; it is there, but the FSDS carries 131 rows in seventeen years, so the SEC companyconcept API is the source and the FSDS rows are the supplement, exactly as F2 did for the cover share count and with the same fetch/parse/build shape. 840 raw files were added, all in the manifest.
- **The count.** The plan says "the cover-page share count as of that date"; the check uses the count the portfolios actually use (`shares.resolve`: cover, then balance sheet, then weighted average, split-scaled, plausibility-guarded) so that the number tested is the pipeline's. `shares_source` says which it was per row (cover for 7,524 of the 8,515 rows with a ratio, balance sheet 471, weighted average 520).
- **`float_scale` is not excluded.** The plan says any flagged (ticker, year) is excluded. 49 of the 62 flags are the filer's `EntityPublicFloat` filed in thousands, millions or billions (GE's $201.5m, eBay's 3e19) with the pipeline's price and count right; excluding General Electric for a year on GE's decimals attribute is not a price-identity exclusion. The carve-out is narrow and tested: the flag must be 100x or more, and the cap must sit inside [0.5, 20] of the same filer's float in another year within two. Chesapeake's 63x (a stale count, with a next-year float that would have put it in band) stays excluded; Exelon's 2x stays excluded. The flag itself is unchanged; the row says `high`/`low` and `float_scale`, and `public_float_summary.csv` counts both.
- **Test fixture.** The plan's "0.0002 (a $2trn cap against a $5bn float)" is float/cap; the fixture uses cap/float = 351 (high) for that case and a count-in-thousands row at 0.001 (low) so both flags are exercised.
- **Review location.** `review/h1.md` as the plan names it; the G1-G5 review is `decisions/review_g1_g5.md`.
