## Open

- **Every long-short series carries a formation row at the window's last month-end** (2026-08-31) with a gross return of exactly 0 and a net return of minus the rebalance cost, e.g. momentum 0.0 / −0.0012 at turnover 0.58. No forward return exists for that month, so the row is a trade that cannot be evaluated, paid for. It sits in the "All months" count (200 for momentum) and in every Sharpe at the fourth decimal. This is the engine's behaviour, not the check's, and this step did not touch it; the fix is in `portfolio.py` (form no portfolio where no member has a forward return, as `min_names` already does for thin months) and needs a rerun of every specification. Decision for the owner.
- **The regenerated figures are typed in six places** (README: 14.7% ×2, 85.3%, 128 ×2; answer: 14.7%) from the report's outputs; nothing generates them. `test_every_number_in_the_answer_is_on_the_page` catches the answer drifting from the site; nothing catches the README.

- **The summary's `gap_pct` moved** (14.64 → 14.71) as a consequence of the rule; the alternative, keeping the untradeable month in the denominator so the headline number stays put, would have been the wrong month count for the sake of a familiar figure.
