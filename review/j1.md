## Not verified

- **The other 45 `float_scale` rows** (GE, eBay, Mattel, Duke ...) were not given overrides; they are already not excluded, so an override would change `float_source` and nothing else. Exelon was the one whose flag was under the scale bar and cost member-months.
- **PLD's closing filing is the 10-Q/A of 2011-09-08**: the companyconcept API attributes the Q2 2011 cover count to the amendment, and whether the original August 10-Q carried the same count was not checked. Three month-ends are excluded either way, which is the plan's bound.
- **AVB's and EA's true histories** were not sought elsewhere; the second-source step is H6.

## Open

- **`STUB_ROWS = 250`** is a threshold: a name that was a member for under a year and delisted could legitimately have fewer rows. None such was refetched.
- **The refetch stored two more raw files** for names the build does not read; the manifest is the record. A later fetch under the whole-universe path would store the same stub under `prices/yfinance/` and displace nothing, since the stubs are the latest file already.

## Against the plan

- **The override carries the float date.** The plan's override row has a `float_date` column and says the override "replaces the XBRL float"; here it replaces the date as well, because Exelon's XBRL context is the fiscal year end while the text names 30 June, and the cap has to be taken where the float was.
- **"Refetch under a fresh as-of date" stored the stubs under `prices/yfinance_stubs/`**, not `prices/yfinance/`: a stub under the build's path would have become the latest file and, for EA, replaced a 6-row stub with a 1-row one, and the build must not read what was fetched to be logged as unusable.
- **The window's closing filing is judged with the monthly close** at the month-end on or before the count's date (the same `px` the check uses), not a daily close on the filing date.
