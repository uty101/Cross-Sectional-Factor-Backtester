**The rules.** One row per removed name (374). Flagged, with `reason` naming which:

| rule | fires when |
|---|---|
| name | yfinance carries a real (alphabetic) name whose token overlap with the Wikipedia name(s) and with every name the registrant filed under is below 0.5 |
| quote | a live quote (not Yahoo's delisted placeholder) in a currency other than USD, of a type other than EQUITY, or on a non-US exchange **when the name does not confirm the company** |
| level | the median over the float dates inside the membership of close × cover count (in the price basis) ÷ `EntityPublicFloat` is outside [0.5, 20] |
| late | the first price is more than 24 months after the later of the membership start and 2008-01-01, is inside the membership, and no level inside the band says the series is merely short |

`no_info` (yfinance returns nothing) is recorded, not flagged; the level and late tests still apply to it.

## Not verified

- **The 108 in-band levels were not read against the cover text**; the band is the same one H1 uses and the distribution (0.91-2.14) is what non-affiliate floats against total counts should give.
- **What the HAR and GR series are.** "906601" and "1040989" are Yahoo's placeholder names; the instrument behind each was not identified and does not need to be.
- **The 33 flags with no priced member-month** were classified by reading `yf_name`; none was checked further, since none touches a result.
- **KG's series being Maiden Holdings'** rests on the 2008-05-06 first print matching Maiden's IPO month and Kestrel Group being Maiden's successor; the level test has no float for King Pharmaceuticals (no cover count in the SEC API file).

## Open

- **AVB and EA.** Both left the index in August 2026 and their yfinance files are stubs: 27 and 6 rows from 2026-07-17 (`data/raw/prices/yfinance/AVB_2026-09-11.csv` is 3 KB). The identity check flags AVB as late (EA has no month-end at all); the real issue is that two large names have no price history in this pipeline and sit in the price gap, which `price_coverage_missing_tickers.csv` shows. A re-fetch under a later as-of date would fill them; not this step's.
- **Names with no float and no cover count** (KG, SII, SUN and 258 others) have no level; for them the name and late tests are all there is. A name that yfinance has reused for a plausibly-priced company with a similar name would pass.
- **Sunoco.** The membership table carries "SunAmerica" for the SUN interval that ends 2012-10-09; that interval is Sunoco Inc. (SunAmerica left in 1998). The identity row now compares against both names; the membership row itself was not corrected (a universe walk matter, with its own evidence file).

## Against the plan

- **The exchange rule.** The plan flags any exchange outside NYSE / Nasdaq / NYSE American. Yahoo files every delisted symbol under exchange `YHD`, quote type `MUTUALFUND`, so the rule as written flags 79 names of which 8 of the 10 with prices are the company's (Aetna, Time Warner, Express Scripts, Andeavor at levels of 1.0). The code is treated as a placeholder: recorded, not evidence. For live quotes the exchange rule fires only when the name does not confirm the company: Signature Bank's name matches and it has traded on the pink sheets since it failed, and its membership months are Signature Bank's. Currency and quote type (an ETF now on the symbol) fire regardless of name.
- **The first-price rule** fires only when the late series overlaps the removed interval (DELL, DOW, CEG, SNDK and Q restart under a new entity after the old interval ended; their series never touch it) and no in-band level says the series is short rather than reused (HOT starts 2015-01 and SCG 2015-07, both at the company's price).
- **A fifth rule, the price level**, is not in the plan. The plan's four rules meet its "done when" (HAR, EP and COL are flagged by them) but miss GR, whose series G5 had already shown to be another company's at $4, and cannot distinguish HAR from Aetna. The level is the one measurement that separates the five wrong series from the 108 right ones, and it is the H1 ratio, not a new quantity.
- **Where the check runs.** `prices.identity_check` is where the plan put it; the call is in the fundamentals build (the shares build, which supplies the floats and the counts, runs after the prices build in `recompute.rebuild`). The first price is taken from the cleaned daily series, so a ticker rule 1 already dropped has no first price and is not late-flagged; those are the 22 above.
- **`wiki_name` and `sec_name` in the CSV** hold every name the ticker carried ("SunAmerica|Sunoco"), `|`-joined; the plan's single `wiki_name` would have compared Sunoco LP with SunAmerica.
- **Review location.** `review/h2.md`; `review/h1.md`'s commit line is filled in with this commit.
